Five skies for a world made of sweets.

Every one of these is painted for the same pack, so they share a vocabulary: spun sugar instead of cirrus, sprinkles instead of dust, candy instead of rock on the horizon. What changes between them is the hour of the day and what that hour does to sugar — floss at dawn, glass at midnight, toffee going molten at dusk.

Pick one and tell me the number. Each preview sits over a blocky candy horizon so you can read it the way you'll actually see it in game — switch the horizon off from the bar above to judge the sky on its own.

    Once you've picked

    1. Cut it to a cubemap. The chosen sky gets rendered out to six faces — top, bottom, north, south, east, west — at 1024×1024 each, seams matched across the edges so the horizon runs continuously all the way around.
    2. Write the sky definition. A textured skybox entry under assets/candyland/sky/ with its fade-in and fade-out ticks set to the window listed on the card, plus pack.mcmeta and the pack icon.
    3. Test it at the right hour. Load it, jump to the middle of its tick window, and check the two things previews always lie about: how the horizon seam reads when you spin, and whether the sky is bright enough that the world below still looks lit.

    These are concept renders drawn in the browser, not the finished texture files — they exist so you can choose a direction before anything gets painted at full resolution. Your pick is remembered in this browser only. If you want two of them, say so; a day sky and a night sky can ship in the same pack and hand off at the tick boundary.
